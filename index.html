Rooted in Community

Nestled in the heart of Seattle, Volunteer Park Cafe & Pantry has been a cornerstone for neighbors and travelers alike. We believe in the power of good food, warm spaces, and genuine connection.

Whether you're stopping by for a morning coffee, picking up provisions from our curated pantry, or settling in for a hearty lunch, our doors are open to welcome you home.
